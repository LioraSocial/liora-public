Delete Your Account

Last updated July 30, 2026

You can delete your Liora account at any time directly from within the app. This page explains how to do that and what happens to your data when you do.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Liora and go to your Profile
  2. Tap Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Make sure you have downloaded or saved anything you want to keep before proceeding.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, all personal information associated with your account is permanently removed or anonymized within 90 days of deletion. This includes things like your name, email address, username, password, profile picture, and any other information tied to your personal account — as well as your login sessions and device information.

This list may grow over time as we add new features, but the principle stays the same: anything that belongs to your personal account gets deleted.

What stays

Projects you created or collaborated on. Projects on Liora are public community spaces that can be built and contributed to by multiple people. Because of that, we can't automatically delete every Project you've been a part of when you leave — doing so could remove something that other people have put time and effort into as well. If you want to remove a Project you own or founded, please delete it manually from within the app before deleting your account. Projects you only contributed to but didn't found will remain untouched.

Content, Topics, and other things you created within Projects. Because these belong to the Projects they were created in — not to your personal account — they remain on the platform after your account is deleted. If you want them removed, please delete them manually before deleting your account.

Once your account is deleted, anything that remains will no longer be associated with your name, username, or any other personal information.

If, after your account is deleted, you believe content that remains still contains personal information about you that you want removed, contact us at contact@liora-app.org and we will review your request.
