Support

We're here to help.

If you have a question, need help with the app, or want to report an issue, there are a couple of ways to reach us.

Discord — Fastest way to get help

The best place to get quick support is our Discord server. You can ask questions, report issues, and connect with the team and community directly.

Join our Discord

Email

Prefer to reach out by email? You can contact us at any time at:

contact@liora-app.org

We'll do our best to get back to you as soon as possible.

Reporting content or accounts

If you'd like to report a piece of content, a Topic, a Project, or a user account that you believe violates our Terms of Use, you can do so directly from within the app using the report button. You can also reach us at contact@liora-app.org if you'd prefer to report something by email.

For more information, see our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
