Child Safety Standards

Last updated July 30, 2026

The safety of minors is one of Liora's highest priorities. This page outlines our standards and practices against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Our commitment

Liora has a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any content that sexualizes, exploits, endangers, or abuses minors in any way. Such content is strictly and permanently prohibited on Liora, and we take immediate action to remove it and report it to the appropriate authorities.

Minimum age

Liora requires all users to be at least 13 years old. By creating an account, users confirm that they meet this requirement. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 13 to create an account. If we discover that a user is under 13, we will remove their account immediately.

Content moderation

Liora employs the following measures to detect, remove, and report child safety violations:

  • All users can report content, Topics, Projects, or accounts that they believe violate our child safety standards directly from within the app using the in-app report button.
  • Reports are reviewed by our team as quickly as reasonably possible, and content that violates our standards is removed promptly.
  • Accounts found to have posted or distributed CSAM are permanently banned from Liora.

Reporting to authorities

Liora complies with all applicable child safety laws. When we become aware of CSAM on our platform, we report it to the relevant national authorities, including the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline at missingkids.org, as required by US law, and to other relevant authorities as required by applicable local law.

How to report a child safety concern

If you encounter content or behavior on Liora that you believe endangers or exploits a child, please report it immediately using one of the following methods:

  • In-app: Use the report button on any Content, Topic, Project, or user profile.
  • Email: Contact us directly at contact@liora-app.org with as much detail as possible.

If a child is in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services first.
